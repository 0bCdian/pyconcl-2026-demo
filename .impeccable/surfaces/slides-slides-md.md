# Deck de la charla (slides/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: PyCon Chile 2026 attendees, intermediate Python developers, projector in a lit room, Spanish. Job: understand which container practices matter most, by measured impact, and join the live demo from their phones. Proof: measurements in README.md (GKE and Podman), Grafana ramp data, the arena demo. Constraints: 30 minutes, ~10–12 of them live demo; order by measured impact; every number sourced; no employer branding; works offline (self-hosted fonts, local assets).

Unresolved: arena URL for the QR and repository URL (configurable placeholders), speaker contact, Q&A time.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The deck is a supermarket package front under Chile's food-labeling law: the bad deployment earns black ALTO EN octagons, each triggered by a measured threshold stated on screen; the good one earns none. It refuses the dark, code-forward tech deck with a title top-left and bullets.

OWN-WORLD: White packaging ground; seals as heavy black octagons with a thin white keyline and white condensed caps; the nutrition-facts table with thick and thin black rules and tabular figures; one red reserved for lost or charged orders; grey only for withdrawn or ghost states. Archivo in heavy and condensed widths for seals and headlines, Archivo for text, JetBrains Mono only for code and measured values in tables.

STORY: Two identical apps; seals appear on A as each practice is measured; the audience's own orders show what the seals cost; each practice in ranking order removes one seal; the close is the table where B carries none.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title top-left in heavy black over two lines; three octagons in a row below it (ALTO EN MEMORIA, ALTO EN WORKERS, ALTO EN PÉRDIDAS); right column the table "Información de despliegue" with A and B values per pod; the QR sits where a package's barcode goes, labeled as the way to join; event line bottom-left.
